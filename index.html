The 5-step way to add AI without the mess

The same order works for a one-person shop and a 50-person team. Skip a step and the tools end up unused.

  1. Map the week

    List repetitive tasks and the hours they take. That list is your opportunity.

  2. Pick one bottleneck

    Choose the task with the most hours and the lowest risk if AI gets it slightly wrong.

  3. Choose one tool

    Match the job to a category, then trial one tool for 14 days. Not five.

  4. Write the playbook

    Save the prompts, rules and checks so anyone on the team gets the same result.

  5. Measure, then stack

    Compare hours before and after. Keep what pays, then add the next layer.

Questions people ask first

Is 67805.com free to use?

Yes. Every tool, guide and calculator is free. The site is funded by advertising, clearly labeled sponsorships and reader support.

Do you get paid to recommend tools?

Editorial picks are never sold. Sponsored listings are always labeled Sponsored and never change our editors’ picks or rankings. Details on how we make money.

What is an AI stack?

A small set of AI tools, one per job, that work together: an assistant for daily drafting, plus tools for support, sales, content, admin or automation depending on your goals.

How much time can AI save a small business?

It depends on how much repetitive work you have. Our estimates use 2 to 6 hours per person per week per area, and the ROI calculator shows the math so you can plug in your own numbers.
