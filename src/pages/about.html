About 67805.com

We help small businesses choose, set up and measure AI tools, with free tools, honest guides and the math shown.

What we do

Most AI advice is written either by vendors selling a product or by enthusiasts who do not run a business. We sit in between. 67805.com publishes a sorted directory of AI tools, interactive planners such as the stack builder, ROI calculator and readiness score, a free prompt library, and practical guides. Everything is free to use.

Why the name

67805 is simply our domain name: short, memorable and easy to type. We like that it reads as a rising run, 6-7-8, followed by our five-step method. It has no other meaning and no connection to any place, phone number or company using the same digits.

How we work

  • Work first, tools second. Every recommendation starts from a business task and the hours it takes.
  • Show the math. Our estimates show their assumptions so you can change them.
  • Separate money from opinion. Sponsored placements are labeled and never change editors’ picks. Read how we make money.
  • Plain English. If a term needs explaining, it goes in the glossary.

Who we are

67805.com is run by a small independent team of operators, writers and developers, and is part of a network of independently operated websites. Guides are written and reviewed by the 67805.com editorial team following our editorial and AI policy.
