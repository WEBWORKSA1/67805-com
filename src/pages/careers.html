How we hire

  1. Send the form with links to your work.
  2. A short paid trial task, scoped to under 3 hours.
  3. A 30-minute video call.
  4. An offer, usually within 2 weeks of applying.

We never ask applicants for payment or bank details during the process. If anyone does in our name, it is a scam: report it to us.
