Business prompt library

30 prompts written for real business jobs. Copy one, replace the parts in [brackets], and paste it into any AI assistant.

Tip: give the assistant context first (who you are, who the audience is, what good looks like), then the task, then the format you want back.

Why these prompts work

Every prompt here follows the same four-part pattern: a role, the context, a specific task and the output format. Constraints such as word limits, banned phrases and “flag anything you are unsure of” cut the generic filler that makes AI writing easy to spot. Most prompts also ask for options rather than one answer, because choosing between three drafts is faster than fixing one.
