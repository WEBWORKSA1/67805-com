Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026.

This policy explains what information 67805.com (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (blueprint request, newsletter signup, contact, tool submission, contest entry, job application, advertising inquiry or support pledge) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email and message, plus the page you submitted from and how you arrived at the site (for example a campaign tag or referring site). We use this information only to respond to you, deliver what you asked for, run contests, consider applications and improve the site.

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through a form-processing service (FormSubmit), which handles the data on our behalf. Donations are processed by PayPal under PayPal’s own privacy policy; we do not see or store your payment card details.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and advertising partners may log technical information such as IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed and time of visit. Interactive tools on this site (stack builder, ROI calculator, readiness score, shortlist) run in your browser. Their inputs are not sent to us unless you choose to email us a result. Settings such as your theme, shortlist and cookie choice are stored locally in your browser.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info and, in the EU, youronlinechoices.eu. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message, managed through Google’s consent tools, before personalized ads are used.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you press play. Once loaded, YouTube may set cookies under Google’s privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site (hosting, form delivery, payments, advertising), when you ask us to introduce you to a partner, or when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries and blueprint requests for up to 24 months, job applications for up to 12 months, and newsletter addresses until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada’s PIPEDA or California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. To make a request, use our contact form and choose “Privacy request”. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Children

This site is intended for adults running or working in businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit the data we collect and who can access it.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use our contact form.
