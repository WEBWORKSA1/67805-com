Editorial and AI policy

How we decide what to publish, how we use AI ourselves, and how we fix mistakes. Last updated October 27, 2026.

Independence

Editorial decisions, including which tools appear, how they are described and which are editors’ picks, are made by our editorial team alone. Advertisers and sponsors cannot buy, review or approve editorial content. Paid placements are labeled Sponsored. See how we make money.

How we research

  • We describe tools from their official documentation, public pricing pages and hands-on use where possible.
  • We avoid claims we cannot check, such as unverified vendor statistics.
  • Pricing models are reviewed regularly, but plans change often, so we always tell readers to confirm on the vendor’s site.
  • Estimates of hours saved are planning figures from our own model. We label them as estimates and show the assumptions.

How we use AI in our own work

We use AI assistants the way we recommend our readers do: for research help, outlines, first drafts and editing suggestions. A human editor decides what is published, checks facts and is responsible for every page. We do not publish unreviewed AI output, and we do not mass-produce pages.

Updates and corrections

Each guide shows the date it was last updated. If you spot an error, tell us through the contact form and choose “Correction or update”. We fix factual errors promptly and note significant corrections on the page.

Third-party content

Videos are embedded from YouTube with their creators credited. We do not copy text from other publishers. Product names and logos belong to their owners; see the trademark notice.
