How to use this calculator well

Start with honest inputs. Track the repetitive work for one normal week before guessing: replying to the same questions, copying data between apps, writing first drafts, formatting reports. Most teams overestimate how much AI will remove and underestimate the setup time, so the defaults here are deliberately conservative: 30% of repetitive work automated and 20 hours to set up and train.

Use the loaded hourly cost, not just the wage. Saved hours only become savings if they go to work that earns money or replaces paid time, such as overtime, contractor hours or a hire you no longer need yet. If the saved time just disappears into the week, count a lower value.

Worked example

Three people each spend 10 hours a week on repetitive tasks. Automating 30% of that saves 3 × 10 × 0.30 × 4.33 = 39 hours a month. At $35 an hour that is $1,364 of gross value. Subtract $120 in tool subscriptions and the net is about $1,244 a month. Setup takes 20 hours, or $700, so payback comes in well under a month.

What this does not include

  • Revenue gains, such as faster replies converting more leads. Treat those as upside.
  • Quality risk. Budget review time for anything customer-facing.
  • Price changes. Re-check tool pricing every renewal.
