An AI content workflow that does not sound like AI

How to use AI to plan, draft and repurpose marketing content while keeping your voice and your facts.

By the 67805.com editorial teamUpdated October 27, 20268 min read

AI can cut content production time dramatically. It can also flood your channels with bland, interchangeable posts that readers skip. The difference is workflow. Used as a first-draft engine inside a clear process, with your expertise feeding it, AI helps you publish more of what only you can say.

The workflow at a glance

  1. Capture raw material that only you have.
  2. Plan topics around real customer questions.
  3. Draft with AI, using your material as the source.
  4. Edit for voice, accuracy and a clear point of view.
  5. Repurpose one piece into many formats.
  6. Measure and feed the results back into planning.

1. Capture raw material

Generic input produces generic output. Feed AI things it cannot find elsewhere: recordings of sales calls, answers you gave customers, notes from projects, your opinions on industry trends. A ten-minute voice memo about a client problem you solved contains more original material than any keyword list.

2. Plan from real questions

Collect the questions customers ask in emails, calls and reviews. Group them into themes, then ask an AI assistant to turn each theme into article, video and social ideas. Add search data from an SEO tool to prioritize topics people actively look for.

3. Draft with your material as the source

Give the assistant your notes or transcript and tell it to use only that material. Ask for a structure first, approve it, then ask for the draft. Constraints help: word limits, reading level, phrases to avoid, and an instruction to mark anything it is unsure of.

Useful instruction: “Use only the facts in my notes. Where you need a fact that is not there, write [NEEDS SOURCE] instead of inventing one.”

4. Edit like a human

This is where your brand is protected. In each draft:

  • Cut the first paragraph if it only warms up.
  • Add one specific example, number or story from your experience.
  • Take a clear position. Bland balance reads as filler.
  • Check every fact, name and statistic.
  • Read it aloud. Rewrite anything you would not say to a customer.

5. Repurpose one piece into ten

A single strong article or video can become a newsletter section, three short social posts, two short-video scripts, a carousel and a quote graphic. AI does this conversion well because the ideas and facts are already approved. The prompt library includes a repurposing prompt.

6. Measure what matters

Track the content that produces leads and replies, not just views. Each month, ask which pieces drove inquiries, and plan more of those. Over time this loop matters more than any tool.

A note on search

Search engines reward content that is helpful, original and written for people, however it is produced. Mass-produced pages with no original value are a risk. Use AI to publish better content faster, not to publish more thin pages.
