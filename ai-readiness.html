How scoring works

Each answer scores 0 to 3. Your total is converted to a score out of 100. Any area where you score 0 or 1 is flagged as something to work on first.

  • 75–100: ready to scale with connected automations
  • 45–74: ready for one or two focused pilots
  • 0–44: lay the groundwork first

Your answers stay in your browser unless you ask for the full report.
